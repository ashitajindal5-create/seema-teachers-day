A Teacher's Day letter
For

Ms. Seema
Davesar

“Some teachers become a lesson.
Some become a memory.
A very few become a part of who we are.”

Ma’am,

I wanted to make something that could hold a simple thank you—but some kinds of gratitude are too big for a single sentence.

Thank you for every lesson, every conversation, every moment of patience, and every time you believed in me. The things a teacher gives us do not always end when the class does. Sometimes they quietly follow us for years.

When I look back, I will remember not only what you taught me, but how it felt to be taught by someone who cared.

Wherever life takes me, a little part of this classroom will always stay with me.

And if I ever become even a little kinder, braver, or better because of something you taught me, then your lesson will still be living on.

Thank you for being one of the beautiful chapters in my story.

With all my love, always.
— Ashita
